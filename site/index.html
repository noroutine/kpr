kpr

keeper for your homelab registry

Push. Pull. Forget.

Just enough on top of your registry to stop your disk — and your life — from bloating. Push with a TTL, pull while it lasts, forget the rest. Your registry, your disk back.

$ docker tag my-app:build my-registry:5000/my-app:10m
$ docker push my-registry:5000/my-app:10m
# …10 minutes later it's due — the next sweep deletes it.

Try it.

Not a drop-in replacement: kpr is not a registry — it keeps one clean and tidy. You run a stock registry:3 beside it on a shared volume; kpr marks, sweeps, and collects around it.

Ten minutes, two containers, no database: quickstart. Already run distribution? bolt it on instead.

# compose.yml + registry-config.yml per the quickstart, then:
$ docker compose up -d
$ crane copy busybox:latest localhost:5000/test/hello:10m
$ docker exec kpr kpr sweep   # dry-run: plans, deletes nothing
